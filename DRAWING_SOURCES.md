# Published drawing sources

The atlas uses published originals and original archive links. It does not redraw project analyses. Image previews are resized/compressed for the web; no content has been redrawn. Authors retain rights to their material. The source and original asset are linked beside each drawing.

Historical reconstructions, design-stage predictions, practice research and district maps are labelled by type, date and scope. A source link does not imply an endorsement by the author.

## Known gaps
