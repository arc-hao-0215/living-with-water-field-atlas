# Field-note editorial review

This revision expands all 18 visited records while preserving the ten unvisited research entries. It is an editorial draft for Hao's review. It does not manufacture personal experiences to make a site sound visited.

Recovered visit history and conversation paraphrases are used only where explicitly supported. Assistant-written itinerary prompts are not treated as observations. Previous generated reports are not treated as independent proof of firsthand experience.

## Highest-value additions from Hao

1. One actual project, question and answer from each professional exchange (MVRDV, PosadMaxwan, TU Delft).
2. Original images with captions naming location, viewpoint and the detail supporting the interpretation.
3. For Waterbuurt West, the specific everyday benefits or difficulties residents described.
4. For Benthemplein, the recorded weather, basin condition and actual activities during the visit.
5. For Maeslantkering and Parksluizen, which operating stages were personally witnessed and what the guides explained.

These gaps are invitations to add evidence, not reasons to substitute invented scenes. Takeaways in this draft are proposed interpretations for author review. Existing atlas dates have not been globally audited; some may reflect itinerary groupings and should be reconciled with dated photographs before publication.
